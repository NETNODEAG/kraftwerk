# Three problems NodeHive solves

::body::
- **Too many tools**: one site per CMS instance, content copied by hand between channels
- **Editors locked out**: developers own the templates, editors wait for a deploy to fix a typo
- **Growth means rebuilds**: a new microsite or app means a new stack, not a new space
