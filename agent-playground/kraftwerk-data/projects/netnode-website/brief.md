# netnode.ch website

Keep netnode.ch fast, accessible and ahead of the competition.

## What done looks like

- Every check workflow (website, accessibility, lighthouse, certificates) runs green on its routine.
- Competitor changes from the competitor-watch bundle are reviewed weekly and turned into tickets where they matter.
- Helpdesk tickets about the site are answered within one working day.

## Constraints

- The site runs on NodeHive; content changes go through the CMS, never through the repository.
- No push to production without a green accessibility audit.

## Stakeholders

- Marketing owns the content, the web team owns the stack.
